How MeetLink works

Passcode and link

Each meeting has one 11-character passcode. The link opens the same meeting. Anyone with either can join.

Expiry

Choose 15 minutes to 24 hours. 60 minutes is the default. The meeting closes for everyone when time is up.

Admin controls

The person who creates the meeting is the admin and chooses whether everyone or only the admin sees the countdown.

Chat and images

Messages and images go to everyone currently in the meeting. Nothing is saved after you leave.

Video and voice

Start a video or voice-only call from the room. Your browser asks for camera or microphone access first.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a meeting link with a passcode?

Open Create, choose an expiry time and who can see it, then press Create meeting. MeetLink generates a passcode and a shareable link instantly.

How long does a meeting last by default?

60 minutes. The admin can pick 15, 30, 120 or 240 minutes, or a custom duration up to 24 hours.

Who can see the expiration time?

The admin decides: everyone in the meeting, or only the admin.

Who can join a meeting?

Anyone with the passcode or the link can join until the meeting expires.

Can I share images in the meeting?

Yes. Images added in the chat appear for everyone currently in the meeting.
